What we work on

Non-viral gene delivery

Magnetic nanocomplexes and lipid carriers that deliver genes into stem cells and cancer cells.

Read more →

Nanomaterials for photoactivated cancer therapy

Gold nanorods and photosensitizer nanocomplexes that turn light into heat or reactive oxygen species inside tumors.

Read more →

3D stem cell culture and injectable matrices

Spheroids, microgels, and injectable hydrogels that keep transplanted stem cells alive and active.

Read more →

Nano-sensors for cancer cell isolation

Biodegradable nano-sensors carrying molecular beacons that find and isolate drug-resistant cancer cells.

Read more →

Why train with us

1

Hands-on, interdisciplinary training

Learn polymer and nanoparticle synthesis, non-viral gene delivery, stem cell culture, and animal disease models in one lab.

2

An international lab

We run TEEP research programs for international interns and have hosted an exchange student from HCMUT in Vietnam. Our department is listed as an English-taught program on NTHU's international admission website.

3

Recognized work

Our work received the National Innovation Award (2020), the National Innovation Excellence Award (2022), the Future Tech Award (2021, 2023), and the Prof. Chau-Jen Lee Research Scholar Award (2026).

Meet Prof. Chang

Chien-Wen (Jeff) Chang is a Professor and the Chair of the Department of Biomedical Engineering and Environmental Sciences at NTHU. He received his PhD from the University of Utah and did postdoctoral research at UC San Diego and UCLA. His research covers drug and gene delivery and stem cell engineering.
