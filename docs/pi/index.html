Principal Investigator

Chien-Wen (Jeff) Chang, Ph.D.

Principal Investigator · Professor and Chair, Department of Biomedical Engineering and Environmental Sciences

Chien-Wen (Jeff) Chang is a Professor and the Chair of the Department of Biomedical Engineering and Environmental Sciences at National Tsing Hua University (NTHU), Taiwan. He received his Ph.D. in Pharmaceutics and Pharmaceutical Chemistry from the University of Utah. He then trained as a CIRM Postdoctoral Fellow in Bioengineering at UC San Diego and a CNSI Postdoctoral Fellow in Chemistry and Biochemistry at UCLA. He previously served as Deputy Director of the Biomedical Science and Engineering Center at NTHU. His research interests include drug and gene delivery, molecular beacons, cell encapsulation, and stem cell engineering. He serves on the Board of Directors of the Biomaterials and Controlled Release Society (BCRS), Taiwan.
