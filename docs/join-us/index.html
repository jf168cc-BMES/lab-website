Who we are looking for

We are recruiting in all four categories below.

MS students

Master's degree in the Department of Biomedical Engineering and Environmental Sciences.

PhD students

Doctoral degree in the same department, working on one of our research themes.

TEEP interns

Research stays of up to six months under Taiwan's TEEP program, funded by the Ministry of Education.

Exchange and visiting students

Students from NTHU partner universities, including VNUHCM University of Science and other partners in Vietnam.

Backgrounds we welcome

Chemistry, chemical engineering, materials science, pharmacy, and biomedical engineering. Coursework in polymers, colloids, or pharmaceutics is a plus.

What you will work on

  • Non-viral gene delivery
  • Nanomaterials for photoactivated cancer therapy
  • 3D stem cell culture and injectable matrices
  • Nano-sensors for cancer cell isolation

How to apply

  1. Email us first. Use the subject line below, attach your CV and transcripts, and tell us in one paragraph which research theme interests you and why.
  2. Apply to our department through the NTHU international admission system at apply.nthu.edu.tw. Please email Prof. Chang before you apply. The lab cannot admit students directly.
  3. Apply for scholarships at the same time. The NTHU scholarship is part of the admission application. The Taiwan Scholarship is applied for separately through Taiwan's office in your country.
  4. For TEEP, see our program page and send Prof. Chang your CV, a one-page statement of your study objectives and planned dates, proof of academic qualifications, and contact details for two references, at least one month before your planned start. A video interview may follow.

Email format

Subject: [MS / PhD / TEEP] Your name – Research theme
Attach: CV and transcripts
Body: one paragraph on which project interests you and why

Living in Hsinchu

Language

Our department is listed as an English-taught program at NTHU. Lab meetings and presentations are in English, and both English and Chinese are used day to day. You can also take Chinese courses at the NTHU Chinese Language Center.

Arrival

Airport transport, residence permit (ARC), health insurance, and bank accounts are explained on the NTHU Campus Life page.

Housing

Dormitories are managed by the NTHU Division of Student Housing. For new international graduate students, the department applies for summer housing on your behalf.

Tuition

Tuition and fees for international students are listed on NTHU's admission website. The NTHU International Student Scholarship waives tuition and credit fees.

Questions in Vietnamese

NTHU offers online consultation in Vietnamese and English on Mondays, 14:00–17:00 (Taiwan time), during the semester.

FAQ

Do I need to speak Chinese?

Our department is listed as an English-taught program, so Chinese is not required for your degree courses, and our lab meetings are held in English. If you want to learn Chinese, NTHU's Chinese Language Center offers courses.

Can I start as a TEEP intern and apply for a degree later?

Yes. TEEP is designed to introduce international students to Taiwan's research environment and encourage further study in Taiwan. Degree admission still goes through NTHU's regular application.

How long does the funding last?

The NTHU International Student Scholarship lasts up to two years for MS students and up to five years for PhD students. It continues each year after a renewal review, and PhD students reapply in their fifth year. The Taiwan Scholarship covers up to two years for a master's degree and four years for a PhD.

How much will my studies cost?

Tuition and fees are listed on NTHU's admission website. If you receive the NTHU International Student Scholarship, tuition and credit fees are waived.
